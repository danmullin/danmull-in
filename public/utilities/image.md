# Image converter

Drop a photo, pick a format, take the file back. Conversion happens in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF can be written when the browser supports them. HEIC and HEIF can be read, not written. An animated GIF becomes a still of its first frame.
