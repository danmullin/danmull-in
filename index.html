About

I’m Dan Mullin — a developer with a long career building for the web, mostly inside other people’s products. That means fewer public case studies and more quiet craft: systems that held up, and taste shaped by shipping.

What I love making on my own time is closer to the metal of joy: programming languages, game feel, and the tools around both. Synth is the flagship. Games are the gravity well. If something here resonates, you’re welcome to poke the repos.
